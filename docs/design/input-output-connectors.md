# Input and output plug connectors

Decision recorded 2026-11-13. J201 is the 48 V input and J301 is the 12 V output. They are different keyed XT sizes; their final board positions are undecided. Neither is a crimp system: the mating cable plugs have solder cups, so there is no crimp contact or crimp tool to buy.

The schematic symbols carry `LCSC`, `Manufacturer`, `Manufacturer Part`, `Footprint`, and `Datasheet` fields. The active footprints are the matching JLC imports; alternative project footprint copies are based on KiCad 10's [XT30PW-M drawing](https://www.tme.eu/Document/6eb2005a51a52592b3f19e8a450c54c8/XT30PW-M.pdf) and [XT60PW-F drawing](https://www.tme.eu/Document/1191bc2fa3aee3c446e5a895fd8f7983/XT60PW-F.pdf), but are not placed on the saved board. JLC-imported STEP and WRL models exist under `hardware/gan-buck-converter/JLCImport.3dshapes`; the active footprints reference the WRL files. The input XT30 WRL arrived with all four materials fully transparent; their transparency was corrected to zero and a KiCad GLB export includes an opaque J201 mesh. Neither model has been visually aligned and verified on the board. Check the exact C431092 and C428722 mechanical drawings and a physical sample against the board before fabrication, especially the XT30-M30 variant and the output mating clearance.

**Rating discrepancy to resolve before fabrication:** [JLCPCB lists C431092](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/XT30PW-M30.G.Y/C431092) at 15 A and 500 V DC. [Amass's 2025 catalog](https://www.china-amass.com/public/upload/20250319/41b7a7787d7c61edc3371469ba98f193.pdf) explicitly includes XT30PW-M30.G.Y in the XT30PW family rated 20 A maximum, 500 V DC, and 100 mating cycles. [Amass's XT30PW-M product page](https://www.china-amass.net/xt30pw-m-product/) instead lists 20 A with 16 AWG at up to 85 K rise, 80 V DC, and 20 mating cycles. An [older XT30PW-M sheet](https://www.tme.eu/Document/6eb2005a51a52592b3f19e8a450c54c8/XT30PW-M.pdf) gives yet another mating-cycle figure. The 48 V nominal input is below both voltage figures, but the conflicting specifications should be confirmed for the exact purchased part. [Amass's XT60PW-F page](https://www.china-amass.net/xt60pw-f-product/) lists 35 A at up to 85 K rise, 80 V DC, and 100 mating cycles; [LCSC describes C428722](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C428722.html) as 30 A. Use the lower 15 A and 30 A catalog current figures for the board plan and confirm the exact revision with Amass or JLCPCB if a rating near the limit matters.

**The active JLC footprints use different pad numbering.** On J201 XT30, pad 2 goes to F201 and pad 1 to `V_IN_RTN` (not board `GND`); pads 3/4 are mounting supports and are no-connect. On J301 XT60, pad 3 goes to `V_OUT` and pad 4 to `GND`; pads 1/2 are mounting supports and are no-connect. The unused `hexagan` alternative footprints use different numbering, so never mix a JLC symbol with an alternative footprint. Check the molded polarity marks on the actual connector before soldering the harness.

The saved PCB currently has both connector footprints near the same right edge, with overlapping courtyards; `12V OUT` silkscreen remains near the left edge. These positions, rotations, body overhang, and the overall placement are **tentative**. Move and validate the output connector and its label before fabrication. The layout is **not fabrication ready**: KiCad reports DRC violations, routing remains incomplete, and the new connector pads still need power-copper routes. Reserve clearance for the fully inserted cable plugs, fingers, insulation and strain relief when finishing layout.

## Cable harnesses

| Board end | Cable mate to buy separately | Cable recommendation | Bench end |
|---|---|---|---|
| J201 XT30 male | [Amass XT30U-F, LCSC C99102](https://www.lcsc.com/product-image/C99102.html), female solder-cup plug | Two 16 AWG flexible wires, red to `+`, black to `-`; fuse and current limit remain on the board and bench supply | Appropriate insulated leads for the 48 V bench supply |
| J301 XT60 female | [Amass XT60H-M, LCSC C5334533](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/plug_Changzhou-Amass-Elec-XT60H-M_C5334533.html), male solder-cup plug with cover; the plain [XT60-M, C98733](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/Connectors_Changzhou-Amass-Elec-XT60Male-head_C98733.html) is another mate | Two 12 AWG flexible wires, red to `+`, black to `-` | Appropriate insulated leads for the electronic load |

The PCB connectors are the only items in the PCBA BOM. The cable plugs and wire must be bought separately from LCSC or another distributor; parts purchased into JLCPCB's assembly library cannot be shipped as loose harness parts. Slide the XT60 cover and heat-shrink over the wires before soldering. Tin the wire and solder cup, make each joint without softening the housing, insulate both contacts, add strain relief, then continuity-check end to end and test for no `+`-to-`-` short. A temperature-controlled soldering iron and suitable solder are needed; **crimp-tool cost is $0** for this choice. Build or buy the two bench adapter leads before first power-on.

Use the **female** plug on the supply cable so an unplugged energized supply does not expose male pins. Keep the 48 V source switched off while mating or unmating J201. The [input hot-plug test](input_supply.md#verification) is a controlled verification step, not normal operation. The [grounding and scope rules](input_supply.md#grounding-and-scope-measurements) still apply: J201 negative is `V_IN_RTN`, separated from board `GND` by Q201.

Snapshot checked 2026-11-13; stock and price can change before ordering. The **two board connectors total $1.0264 per board in listed component cost** at 1+, excluding assembly and other costs. JLCPCB's [published assembly fee table](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-assembly-price) lists $3.58 hand-soldering labor per order, $0.0164 per manually assembled joint, and $3.07 per Extended part number for Economic feeder loading when applicable. The two footprints have eight plated holes total (four electrical and four mechanical), so counting every hole would be $0.1312 per board in joint labor; confirm the charged joint count and any feeder loading in the order preview. The overall PCBA setup, stencil, PCB fabrication, tax and shipping are separate. Include both JLCPCB part numbers in the BOM and select wave-solder assembly for both in the order preview. Buy the mates separately and budget wire, insulation and soldering supplies.

Amass's 2025 catalog includes the exact XT30PW-M30.G.Y variant in a **100-cycle** XT30PW family, but its XT30PW-M product page says **20 cycles**; an older XT30PW-M sheet says **1000**. The XT60PW-F product page says **100 cycles**. These are conflicting published qualification figures, not a prediction that a connector fails on the next insertion. Ask Amass or JLCPCB which figure applies to C431092 before using it for frequent daily reconnection. Replace a connector that becomes loose or hot. Do not treat a catalog figure as a board qualification result.
