## Purpose and requirements

Convert the 48 V bus to the 12 V output with a synchronous buck: two discrete enhancement-mode GaN FETs, a GaN half-bridge gate driver, the output inductor and capacitors, and the current-sense shunt.

Targets are in [specifications.md](../specifications.md); boundaries and interface signals are in the **Power stage** section of [schematic-hierarchy.md](../schematic-hierarchy.md).

**Requirements**

- 48 V in, 12 V out at up to 12.5 A (150 W), with parts and copper rated for 16.7 A (200 W).
- Switch at 400 kHz nominal, with every part sized to run anywhere in 300–500 kHz.
- Meet the efficiency target in the specifications with natural-convection cooling. The component-level estimate is 97.9 % for this stage at 150 W, 48 V, 400 kHz (about 3.2 W of loss), before layout losses.
- Keep the GaN gate voltage within the FETs' limits under all conditions, including ringing.
- Support the shortest safe dead time, set in the MCU's ePWM.
- Meet the output ripple and load-transient targets in the specifications.
- Provide a Kelvin-connected current-sense shunt whose value lets sensing place the overcurrent threshold within 0–3 V.
- Hold both gates off when the driver is unpowered or `HS_PWM` / `LS_PWM` are undriven. The LM5113-Q1's HI / LI must not float; its datasheet specifies 100–300 kΩ internal input pull-downs. On this board the 10 kΩ pull-downs R609 / R610 on the enable sheet's `HS_PWM` / `LS_PWM` outputs provide a stronger default-low bias before `+3V3` powers U603 (see open questions; input-filter delay must be measured at the gates).

## Calculations

One row per calculation. Note where each input came from (specification, datasheet, measurement) in Notes.

| Calculation | Formula | Result | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duty cycle | $D = V_{out} / V_{in}$ | $12 / 48 = 0.25$ | Ideal; real duty is slightly higher to cover losses |
| High-side conduction loss | $I_{out}^2 \cdot R_{DS(on),hot} \cdot D$ | $12.5^2 \cdot 5\,m\Omega \cdot 0.25 \approx 0.20$ W | $R_{DS(on)}$ 3.3 mΩ max at 25 °C (datasheet) × ~1.5 assumed when hot; confirm the factor from the datasheet curve |
| Low-side conduction loss | $I_{out}^2 \cdot R_{DS(on),hot} \cdot (1-D)$ | $12.5^2 \cdot 5\,m\Omega \cdot 0.75 \approx 0.59$ W | Same assumptions |
| Output-charge loss | $\tfrac{1}{2} Q_{oss} V_{in} f_{sw}$ | $0.5 \cdot 43\,nC \cdot 48 \cdot 400\,kHz \approx 0.41$ W | $Q_{oss}$ 43 nC typ at 50 V (datasheet); $f_{sw}$ = 400 kHz planned |
| Dead-time conduction loss | $V_{SD} \cdot I_{out} \cdot 2 t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw}$ | $2.4 \cdot 12.5 \cdot 20\,ns \cdot 400\,kHz \approx 0.24$ W | $V_{SD}$ 2.4 V typ (datasheet); 10 ns dead time per edge assumed |
| High-side turn-on gate current (plateau) | $(V_{DD} - V_{plateau}) / (R_{pullup} + R_{HOH} + R_{G})$ | $(5 - 2.6) / (2.1 + 2 + 0.5) \approx 0.52$ A | 2.1 Ω driver pull-up, 0.5 Ω FET internal $R_G$, 2.6 V plateau (datasheets); 2 Ω HOH (decision #5). Peak at 0 V gate: $5 / 4.6 \approx 1.1$ A, inside the driver's 1.2 A |
| Turn-on switching time | $Q_{sw} / I_{G}$ | $3.3\,nC / 0.52\,A \approx 6.3$ ns | $Q_{sw}$ 3.3 nC typ (datasheet). Common-source inductance (no Kelvin pin) slows this further |
| Turn-on dv/dt | $V_{in} / (Q_{gd} / I_{G})$ | $48 / (2.6\,nC / 0.52\,A) \approx 10$ V/ns | About 17 V/ns with 0 Ω. LM5113-Q1 HS slew limit is 50 V/ns |
| Turn-on overlap loss | $\tfrac{1}{2} V_{in} I_{valley} t_{sw} f_{sw}$ | $0.5 \cdot 48 \cdot 10.8 \cdot 6.3\,ns \cdot 400\,kHz \approx 0.66$ W | High-side hard turn-on; valley current $12.5 - 3.3/2$ A. About 0.37 W with 0 Ω |
| Turn-off overlap loss (upper bound) | $\tfrac{1}{2} V_{in} I_{peak} t_{off} f_{sw}$ | $0.5 \cdot 48 \cdot 14.2 \cdot 1.4\,ns \cdot 400\,kHz \approx 0.19$ W | $t_{off} = Q_{sw} / (2.6\,V / 1.1\,\Omega)$; the load current charges $C_{oss}$, so the real loss is lower |
| Total FET loss | Sum of the above | ≈ 2.3 W | 0.79 W conduction + 0.41 W $Q_{oss}$ + 0.24 W dead time + 0.85 W overlap. Against a ~6 W total budget at 150 W |

Far below the 1.2 V minimum threshold, which is why the turn-off paths have no added resistance. **Optimistic:** this counts only $C_{rss}$ current through the pull-down, not common-source or gate-loop inductance. Infineon's evaluation board, with an active Miller clamp, shows the low-side $V_{GS}$ reaching roughly +1.8 V during high-side turn-on (UG122052 Figure 25, 48 V → 12 V, 500 kHz, 20 A; read from the plot). Verification test 12 measures ours |
| FET loss split | High side: conduction + $Q_{oss}$ + overlap. Low side: conduction + dead time | High side $0.20 + 0.41 + 0.85 \approx 1.46$ W; low side $0.59 + 0.24 \approx 0.83$ W | 150 W, 400 kHz, from the rows above. In hard-switched turn-on, the $Q_{oss}$ energy of the low-side FET is dissipated in the high-side FET's channel |
| FET junction rise, still air | $P \cdot R_{th(j\text{-}a)}$ | High side $1.46 \cdot 38 \approx 55$ K; low side $0.83 \cdot 38 \approx 32$ K | $R_{th(j\text{-}a)}$ 38 K/W typ on a JEDEC 2s2p (4-layer) board, vertical, still air (exact IGC033S10S1 datasheet). ## Layout notes

What the layout owner must know: loops to keep small, parts to place first, keep-outs, thermal needs.

- The IGC033S10S1 has **no separate Kelvin source pin**. Return each gate loop to the source pad nearest that FET's gate pin, and route the power current away from that spot. On the PG-TSON-6 that is pin 6, the source pad directly beside the gate (pin 1).
- Place the LM5113-Q1 right beside the FETs, with its bootstrap capacitor at its HB/HS pins and its VDD decoupling at its VDD pin.
- Gate margin is about 0.5 V (5 V drive, 5.5 V recommended max), so gate-loop inductance must be as small as possible.
- Put C313 (bootstrap) and C311 (100 nF VDD) on the same side as the driver, right at its pins, with no vias between them and the pins.
- Keep the bootstrap charging loop (C311 → D301/R304 → C313 → low-side FET → back) short.
- Connect the driver's VSS to the low-side FET's source by a short, direct path. If a shunt ever sits in that source path, VSS goes on the FET side of it.
- TI's layout examples assume 0402 parts; 0402 for R305 and C313 would tighten the gate loop if space is short.
- **Power loop first:** C304–C307 (100 nF 0603) go directly across the high-side drain and low-side source, then C301–C303 just outside them. Mark them `Layout = power loop, place first`.
- Place R301 between L301 and the output capacitors, and route `I_SH_OUT_P` / `I_SH_OUT_N` as a tight pair from the shunt's inner pad edges (Kelvin), away from the switch node. Place NT301 and NT302 against the inner edge of their shunt pads; they keep layout from tapping the power copper anywhere else.
- Put the snubber (R303, C310) across the low-side FET, drain to source, inside the power loop.
- Keep the switch-node copper only as large as L301's pad needs; it radiates.

**Power-loop layout (decision #19).** References: [EPC WP010](../datasheets/epc-wp010-optimizing-pcb-layout.pdf) for the method, [Infineon UG122052](../datasheets/infineon-ug122052-igc033s10s1-half-bridge-eval.pdf) (section 6.2) for a working 4-layer layout with this exact FET, and [EPC's PCB layout rules for converters (2025)](../datasheets/epc-pcb-layout-rules-2025.pdf) for gate-loop, via and probing practice.

- **4 layers.** Inner layer 1 is the power loop's return path: a solid, unbroken `GND` pour directly under Q301, Q302 and C301–C307. No traces or splits cross it in that area; a single cut breaks the field cancellation the layout depends on.
- **Stackup.** Use the JLC04161H-1080 4-layer option with 0.0764 mm L1–L2 prepreg and a solid inner-layer return plane beneath the GaN loop. In WP010, this plane spacing is the critical stackup dimension for the chosen optimal loop. The JLC nominal 1.6 mm ordering option calculates to 1.5182 mm of copper and dielectric; check mechanical tolerance at order time. The dimensions are recorded in [specifications.md](../specifications.md).
- **Inner copper is 0.5 oz.** That is fine for the high-frequency return, which spreads across the plane, but no DC load current goes through the inner layers. Keep the 12.5 A path from the switch node to L301 on the outer layers (on EPC's board the switch node drops through inner layers; ours must not).
- **Interleaved vias.** Q302's pads 2–5 alternate drain / source / drain / source (three 0.45 × 3.30 mm inner bars and one 0.65 × 3.30 mm outer bar), the same interleaved structure as EPC's LGA parts. Place alternating `SW_NODE` and `GND` via rows aligned with those bars, between the two FETs. Avoid via-in-pad unless the vias are plugged and capped, which is a process option to check against the Economic PCBA rules first.
- **Input caps.** Put the positive terminals of C304–C307 right at Q301's drain bars, as WP010 does. Split the four parts along the drain and return bars to shorten each current loop; their schematic spacing does not define PCB placement. Infineon's board uses a separate group of 5 × 220 nF 0603 caps at the half-bridge.
- **Low-side gate loop is the most sensitive loop on the board.** LOH / LOL to Q302's gate and the driver's VSS to Q302's pin 6 as short and as tightly paired as possible. Infineon's board shows a low-side gate bump near +1.8 V even with an active Miller clamp, and the LM5113-Q1 has none.
- **Probe points.** The switch node rings at a few hundred MHz, so probe loops must stay under 10 mm (UG122052 section 3.2). Place TP_LSG301 (`LS_DRIVE`) and TP_LSS301 (`GND`) as a 1 mm exposed-pad pair within a few millimetres of Q302 gate pin 1 and source pin 6. Keep the gate pad's branch short and off the driver-to-gate path; return the source pad directly to pin 6, not to a distant ground pour. Confirm the intended scope tip and ground spring can contact both pads without touching neighboring copper or an optional heatsink.
- **Q301 / Q302 footprint.** The library footprint now separates `F.Cu`, `F.Mask`, and `F.Paste` per Infineon's [IGC033S10S1 footprint drawing](https://www.infineon.com/assets/row/public/documents/24/49/infineon-igc033s10s1-datasheet-en.pdf) (Figure 2).. Place from the updated library. Before ordering, confirm the inferred copper overhang, mask registration, and paste pattern for the assembler's stencil thickness. The symbol pin map remains gate 1, drain 2/4, source 3/5/6.

**Thermal provisions (decision #20).**

- Thermal vias under the FET drain and source pads into the inner and bottom pours are the main planned heat path without a heatsink. The exact IGC033S10S1 datasheet gives $R_{th(j\text{-}c)}$ of **0.5 K/W typ at the top** and **1.9 K/W typ at the bottom**; the former does not imply a low junction-to-ambient resistance without a fitted heatsink.
- Place C304–C307 at Q301's drain bars for the shortest input loop before fitting an optional top-side heatsink. Reserve a thermal contact area over Q301 / Q302 and room for mounting hardware outside the input-current loop, interleaved vias and gate/probe access. The heatsink's fins or base may overhang nearby capacitors only with verified underside clearance, electrical insulation and, if needed, a relieved base; do not move the capacitors away to create an empty heatsink square. No mounting-hole pattern is selected yet. The exposed top silicon is internally connected to source: Q301's top is at `SW_NODE` and Q302's at `GND`. An electrically insulating thermal interface must isolate both from the heatsink; the top is not an electrical connection pad.
- The bottom side stays free of parts (single-sided assembly), so a bottom-side heatsink over the thermal vias is a second option. The vias there sit at `V_IN`, `SW_NODE` and `GND` potential, so it also needs an isolating pad.

**Top heatsink clearance check (open until PCB placement and assembled-height measurement).** The [IGC033S10S1 package drawing](https://www.infineon.com/assets/row/public/documents/24/49/infineon-igc033s10s1-datasheet-en.pdf) (Figure 1) specifies a **maximum** body height of 1.032 mm but no minimum. Figure 3 in that datasheet shows the shipping carrier tape: its 1.3 mm and 0.3 mm cross-section dimensions do not specify the mounted FET height. The [CL10B104KC8NNNC data](https://product.samsungsem.com/mlcc/CL10B104KC8NNN.do) gives a capacitor height of 0.80 ± 0.10 mm (0.90 mm maximum). Infineon's [same-FET fixed-stop spreader](https://www.infineon.com/assets/row/public/documents/24/44/infineon-coolgan-motor-drive-reference-board-for-battery-powered-foc-applications-1-usermanual-en.pdf) uses a 0.5 mm insulating pad compressed to a 0.20–0.30 mm device-to-spreader gap; that is a reference design, not a qualified gap for this PCB. For any flat base over the capacitors, the vertical clearance is

$$c=(h_{\mathrm{FET}}+s_{\mathrm{FET}})+g-(h_{\mathrm{cap}}+s_{\mathrm{cap}}),$$

where $s$ is the assembled solder standoff and $g$ is the compressed pad gap. Substituting **both published maximum body heights**, equal solder standoffs and the reference design's 0.20 mm minimum gap gives $c=1.032+0.20-0.90=0.332$ mm. This is only an illustration, **not a worst-case clearance**: the FET minimum height and solder-height spread are unknown. A flat overhanging base is therefore not cleared for this design. After placement, measure the assembled FET and capacitor top heights, model the sink and fasteners in 3D, and use a raised contact island or relief above the capacitors if the required clearance and insulation are not proven. Check that the final sink does not block the Q302 gate probe pads. Keep the four input capacitors in their short-loop positions.

## Open questions

- Confirm the hot $R_{DS(on)}$ factor (1.5× assumed) from the datasheet's normalized curve.
- Check the SLB2212P6R8MTT datasheet for how $I_{sat}$ is defined and for core-loss data near 400 kHz.
- The load-transient target in the specifications is still [TBD]; the simulation's 10 kHz loop gives a dip of about 280 mV on a 10 A step.
- **Output polymers (decision #24).** The catalog gives only a maximum ESR, and the loop's worst corner depends on how far below it the fitted parts sit (2.5 mΩ for the pair is assumed). Measure the ESR or the loop (SFRA) on the first board. The imported `JLCImport:APXJ160ARA271MF80J` pads (3.5 × 1.2 mm, 1.84 mm apart) agree with the catalog's F80 package drawing (terminal span 7.2 mm, gap 1.9 mm, leads 0.5–0.8 mm wide); NCC's catalog does not give a recommended land pattern, so confirm against its product specification or with the assembler.
- **Startup sequence validation.** The [LM5113-Q1 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm5113-q1.pdf) §7.3.2 specifies VDD before the half-bridge VIN bus, while this board derives `+5V` from `V_IN` when J202 is absent. TI's [LM5113 evaluation board guide](https://www.ti.com/lit/ug/snva484b/snva484b.pdf) describes a single-VIN-supply startup, so the practical applicability of that sequence instruction to this topology needs confirmation. Firmware will hold PWM low until the driver rail is stable, but that does not make VDD rise before the bus. Test 5 covers the normal single-bus cold start as well as J202-first operation; a J202-first-only bench procedure does not validate normal operation. R609 / R610 hold HI / LI low, and the driver's VDD UVLO pulls HOL / LOL low, but no fitted gate-to-source resistor independently holds either FET off if the driver is unpowered.
- Gate hold-off with `HS_PWM` / `LS_PWM` undriven relies on the enable sheet's pull-downs (R609 / R610). For the universal control board, where PWM will arrive over a connector, consider 10 kΩ pull-downs at the driver's HI / LI on this sheet.
- **High-frequency input caps:** decision #22 chooses four 0603 parts. Recheck C15725 stock and the exact-part bias curve before assembly release; the capacitance estimate is typical. Confirm all four can fit in the short power-loop placement without stretching the loop.
- **Low-side gate probe point:** TP_LSG301 / TP_LSS301 implement the schematic interface. Confirm the physical pad spacing and scope-probe clearance during placement, before routing the gate loop.
- **Stackup.** JLCPCB JLC04161H-1080 has been entered in the board and specifications: nominal 1.6 mm ordering option, 0.0764 mm L1–L2 dielectric, 0.035 mm outer copper and 0.0152 mm inner copper. JLC's calculated copper-plus-dielectric thickness is 1.5182 mm, while KiCad includes modeled solder mask for 1.5382 mm. Confirm that this exact option and copper weights are selected in the fabrication order (decision #19).
- **Heatsink.** Before PCB fabrication, decide whether Rev A must accept a bolt-on top heatsink. If so, select the sink or adapter mounting pattern, then add its dedicated holes and mechanical/electrical keepouts during PCB layout; H101–H104 are the separate M3 board-mounting holes, not a heatsink pattern.
